[Notra](https://www.usenotra.com) is a generative engine optimization (GEO) tool that asks ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini the questions your buyers ask. It shows whether you appear, who appears instead and how to fix it.

[Notra Sites](https://www.usenotra.com/docs/sites/overview) turns MDX files in your GitHub repository into a hosted blog and changelog on your own domain. It has a built-in Databuddy integration, so you don't need to add a script tag yourself.

## How to add Databuddy to Notra

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Client ID">
    Open your [Databuddy dashboard](https://app.databuddy.cc/websites), select the website that represents your Notra site and copy the **Client ID** from the tracking setup screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add it in Notra">
    In Notra, go to **Sites** in Studio, select your site, open **Integrations** and click **Add Databuddy**. Paste your Client ID into **Client ID** and switch on any of the **Web vitals**, **Errors**, **Outgoing links** and **Interactions** toggles you want.

    If you manage your site from a repository, you can add the integration to `blog.json` instead:

    <CodeBlock
      language="json"
      filename="blog.json"
      code={`{
  "$schema": "https://usenotra.com/schemas/blog.json",
  "name": "Acme",
  "integrations": {
    "databuddy": {
      "clientId": "YOUR_CLIENT_ID",
      "trackWebVitals": true,
      "trackErrors": true,
      "trackOutgoingLinks": true
    }
  }
}`}
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and verify">
    Notra saves your settings as a draft of `blog.json`. Click **Review and publish**, create the pull request and merge it. Once the production deployment finishes, visit your live site and watch real-time visitors appear in your [Databuddy dashboard](https://app.databuddy.cc).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configuration options

You can set every tracker option in `blog.json`, including `trackAttributes`, `samplingRate`, `skipPatterns`, `maskPatterns` and a custom `scriptUrl` or `apiUrl` if you proxy Databuddy through your own domain. The [Notra Databuddy guide](https://www.usenotra.com/docs/sites/integrations/analytics/databuddy) lists every option with its default.

Notra loads the tracker on every live page and adds Databuddy's script and ingestion hosts to your site's Content-Security-Policy, including any custom `scriptUrl` or `apiUrl`, so a proxied setup needs no extra configuration.

## Custom event tracking

With `trackAttributes` on, you can track clicks by adding a `data-track` attribute to links and buttons in your MDX pages. Databuddy sends the `data-track` value as the event name and the element's other `data-*` attributes as properties:

<CodeBlock
  language="html"
  code={`<a href="/pricing" data-track="pricing_clicked" data-placement="changelog-footer">
  See pricing
</a>`}
/>

## Troubleshooting

<Callout type="info">
**No data showing up**

1. Make sure you merged the pull request and the production deployment finished. Previews and local development don't send events, so test on the live site.
2. Check that the domain on your Databuddy website matches your Notra site's domain.
3. Test in an incognito window to rule out ad blockers and previous opt-outs.
</Callout>
